DFS Exhaustive

Pure backtracking depth-first search. Guaranteed to find a solution if one exists. Exponential worst-case time O(N!) — feasible only for small N. Uses implicit call stack memory. For large N (≥30), a timeout of 5 seconds is applied.

Greedy Hill-Climbing

Places queens column by column, always choosing the row with minimum conflicts. Very fast O(N²) construction but can get stuck in local optima. Runs a random-restart loop to escape local minima. Often finds approximate solutions for large N quickly.

Simulated Annealing

Starts with a random permutation, then iteratively swaps queen positions, accepting worse solutions with probability e^(-ΔE/T). Temperature cools geometrically. Balances exploration and exploitation — usually finds exact solutions up to N=500.

Genetic Algorithm

Population of N-permutations evolves via tournament selection, order-crossover (OX), and mutation. Fitness = N*(N-1)/2 − conflicts. Elitism preserves best individuals. Scales well; typically converges within hundreds of generations for large N.
